Leadership development

Prepare to lead before the badge arrives.

Company leadership is more than tactics. Build credibility, communicate expectations, manage risk, develop people, document decisions, and create a crew that can perform when conditions are difficult.

Progression

Use the stage you are actually in.

Before promotion

Become dependable

Master your current assignment, teach without ego, learn policy, request acting opportunities, and ask for specific feedback.

Acting officer

Practice the full job

Run roll call, assign work, lead drills, conduct size-ups, document issues, and learn when to elevate a concern.

New company officer

Create clarity

Set crew expectations, learn each member, build a training rhythm, protect psychological safety, and follow through consistently.

Experienced officer

Develop replacements

Coach emerging leaders, improve systems, study near misses, share decision-making, and leave the company stronger.

Command development

Think beyond one company

Strengthen incident organization, communications, resource management, strategic forecasting, and multi-company coordination.

Training leadership

Make learning transfer

Use clear objectives, realistic practice, safe failure, useful evaluation, remediation, and documentation.

Core disciplines

The work behind good leadership.

Crew expectations

Explain what good performance looks like, what is non-negotiable, how feedback works, and how members can raise concerns.

Difficult conversations

Address observable behavior early, explain impact, listen, agree on next steps, document appropriately, and follow policy.

Risk decisions

Separate life-safety urgency from avoidable haste. State strategy, communicate changing conditions, and reassess resources.

After-action learning

Discuss what was expected, what occurred, why it differed, what should continue, and what will change next time.
