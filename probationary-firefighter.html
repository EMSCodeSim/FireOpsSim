The probation rule

Learn your department first.

Departments differ in staffing, apparatus, hose loads, riding positions, tactics, EMS role, terminology, and probation requirements.

  • Department SOP/SOG: authority
  • Officer/task book: required standard
  • FireOpsSim: practice and organization

Never replace department-specific training or supervision with a generic online checklist.

What makes a good rookie

The habits nobody should have to keep reminding you about

Skill matters. Reliability, awareness, attitude, and preparation determine whether the crew trusts you.

01

Be ready before you are needed

Know your assignment, PPE, SCBA, radio, riding position, and apparatus before the tones drop.

02

Learn where everything lives

Do not stop at compartment names. Know what the tool is for, what goes with it, and what you would grab for common calls.

03

Listen before showing what you know

Ask good questions, accept correction without excuses, and learn how your company actually operates.

04

Finish the whole job

The call is not over when the fire is out. Reload, clean, document, restock, refuel, decon, and make the company ready again.

05

Know when to speak up

Safety concerns, lost orientation, low air, equipment problems, changing conditions, and patient deterioration are not times to stay quiet.

06

Own your weak areas

Ask for repetitions before a weakness becomes visible on the worst call of your probation.
