Pump math

Pump discharge pressure practice for real fireground layouts.

Build the habit of identifying the governing line, accounting for losses and then checking what changes when the incident changes.

Practice loop

  • Identify nozzle/device pressure.
  • Estimate friction loss for the actual hose and flow.
  • Add or subtract elevation as appropriate.
  • Account for appliance/system loss when required.
  • Reassess after additional lines open.
