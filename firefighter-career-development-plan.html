Career planning

Build a firefighter career development plan around a real target role.

A useful plan balances training, education, relevant experience and continuing education instead of collecting random certificates.

Plan by quarter

  • Choose one target role and target date.
  • Identify required and recommended credentials.
  • Choose experience you can deliberately seek on shift.
  • Schedule practice, reading and formal training.
  • Review evidence and adjust every 90 days.
