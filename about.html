About FireOpsSim.

FireOpsSim is a firefighter-built project hub focused on practical fireground training tools, pump calculations, and scenario-based learning.

View projectsView all FireOpsSim projects

Mission

FireOpsSim exists to make practical fire training tools easier to find, easier to use, and easier to share with crews. The first focus is driver/operator training because pump pressure math and realistic scenario practice are common needs for firefighters, engineers, instructors, and students.

The long-term goal is to build a connected ecosystem of fire apps and web tools: FireOps Calc for pump calculations, FirePumpSim for scenario practice, printable worksheets for instructors, and future simulation modules for fireground decisions.

What makes the project different

Built around real training problems

The site is organized around firefighter tasks, not generic app marketing.

Designed to grow

Future fire projects can be added as cards, pages, and app links without starting over.

Search-friendly structure

Every major project has its own crawlable landing page, descriptive headings, and internal links.
