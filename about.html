The creator

Built by David Gallaher

David is an experienced firefighter, paramedic, driver/operator instructor, and fire/EMS educator. FireOpsSim grew from a simple goal: make practical training tools easier for firefighters and instructors to use on a phone, tablet, or station computer.

The tools are designed to support training discussions, classroom practice, and station drills. They are not a replacement for certified instruction, department SOPs, manufacturer guidance, adopted codes, or the authority having jurisdiction.

Review approach

Clear formulas and visible limitations

  • Training formulas are shown whenever practical.
  • Operational assumptions are identified in the tool.
  • Department values and SOPs always take priority.
  • Corrections and firefighter feedback guide improvements.

What FireOpsSim includes

The website includes pump-pressure and friction-loss calculators, water-supply tools, visual pump scenarios, printable worksheets, firefighter skills references, HazMat planning aids, inspection resources, and links to FireOps Calc and FirePumpSim.
