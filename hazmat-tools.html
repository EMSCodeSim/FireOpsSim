FAQ

HazMat tool questions

Are these tools a replacement for the ERG?

No. These pages are built to support training and quick organization. Responders should use current ERG guidance, SDS information, department SOPs, dispatch information, product specialists, and HazMat team direction for actual incidents.

Why include calculators?

Simple calculators help with training and planning: spill volume, water needs for decon, and mapping dimensions once a responder has the correct isolation distances from approved references.

HazMat training note: These tools are for education, pre-planning, drill support, and quick estimating. They do not replace the Emergency Response Guidebook, Safety Data Sheets, department SOPs, incident command, product specialists, or local HazMat team direction.
