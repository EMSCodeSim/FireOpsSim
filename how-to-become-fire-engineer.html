Engineer path

How to become a fire engineer: learn the apparatus and the consequences of every decision.

Requirements vary by department, but strong engineers combine driving, placement, apparatus knowledge, water supply, pump operations and calm communication.

Development path

  • Master daily apparatus inspection.
  • Practice placement before focusing only on the pump panel.
  • Build fast, explainable hydraulics habits.
  • Train hydrants, drafting, relay and master streams.
  • Seek supervised driving and acting opportunities allowed by your department.
