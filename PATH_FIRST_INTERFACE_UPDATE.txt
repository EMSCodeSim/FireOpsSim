FireOpsSim Certification Tracker - Path-First Interface Update

- Replaced the busy career-path toolbar with large role-selection cards.
- Selecting a desired role immediately shows its certification/class checklist.
- Previously completed/current certifications are automatically checked from existing local tracker data.
- Core-path completion percentage appears beside the checklist.
- Best next certification is highlighted automatically.
- Optional development items are hidden by default and can be revealed with one button.
- Certification detail editing remains available for issue/expiration dates.
- Full library filters and backup tools are collapsed under Advanced sections to reduce clutter.
- Existing localStorage keys/data remain unchanged for backward compatibility.
