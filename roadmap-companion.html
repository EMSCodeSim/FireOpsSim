FireOpsSim accepts Roadmap's stable certification and task IDs. Example:

/taskbook-resources.html?cert=driver_operator_pumper&task=do_pumper_hydrant_ops&state=CO&source=roadmap

The page still works when only cert is supplied, and gracefully falls back to the full resource hub for a new or custom Taskbook item.
