FireOps Career Road

Support

FireOps Career Road is a local-first career planning and professional-record app. The current iOS release works without creating an account or connecting a department service.

Common fixes

  • A Quick Log entry is not where expected: open Log and use the matching filter, including TASK BOOK for Task Book evidence.
  • Personal data is missing after changing devices: restore a Career Portfolio backup created on the previous device. Personal career data is not automatically cloud-synced.
  • Export or backup cannot be opened: save the file locally first, then reopen it from the app's Backup & Restore workflow.
  • A resource link is unavailable: verify the current requirement with the applicable certifying authority, academy, department, or training provider.
  • Need a clean start: Settings → Reset App erases local career data after confirmation.

Data and backups

Your personal Career Road data stays on the device unless you choose to create, save, print, or share an export or backup. Keep important backup files somewhere you control before resetting the app or changing devices.

Patient information

Do not use FireOps Career Road to store patient-identifying information. It is not an ePCR, exposure-reporting system, or official patient record.

Report a problem

For a reproducible software issue, you can use the public FireOps Career Road issue tracker. Do not include patient information, department-sensitive information, passwords, or private personnel records in a public issue.
