FireOps Career Road

Support

FireOps Career Road is a local-first career planning and professional-record app with an optional ResponderRoadmap department connection.

Common fixes

  • Department Task Books are not updating: open My Department and use Refresh/Sync. Confirm the same account works on ResponderRoadmap.
  • Department sign-in fails: confirm your department account is active, then disconnect and reconnect the department connection.
  • Personal data is missing after changing devices: restore a Career Portfolio backup created on the previous device. Personal career data is not automatically cloud-synced.
  • Export or backup cannot be opened: save the file locally first, then reopen it from the app's Backup & Restore workflow.
  • Need a clean start: Settings → Reset App erases local career data after confirmation.

Department records

Official department Task Book assignments, submissions, reviewer actions, and approvals are managed through ResponderRoadmap. Personal Career Road information remains separate unless you explicitly submit information to a department Task Book.

Report a problem

For a reproducible software issue, you can use the public FireOps Career Road issue tracker. Do not include patient information, department-sensitive information, passwords, or private personnel records in a public issue.
