How to use this tracker

1. Pick the career path closest to your goal. 2. Mark what you already hold. 3. Add issue and expiration dates when applicable. 4. Add department/state-specific classes. 5. Review the expiration dashboard at least monthly.

Certification names, prerequisites, renewal periods, and accepted providers vary by jurisdiction and department. Verify your actual requirements with your AHJ, department, state fire certification authority, EMS authority, and current standards.
