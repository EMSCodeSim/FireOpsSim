Probation

A probationary firefighter training plan for the first 100 shifts.

Build trust through consistent station habits, apparatus knowledge, district learning, skills, fitness, communication and coachability.

First-year priorities

  • Know the apparatus and equipment locations.
  • Learn first-due streets, buildings and water supply.
  • Repeat basic firefighter skills until they are dependable.
  • Ask for feedback and correct small issues quickly.
  • Keep a professional record of training and milestones.
