Incident Command

ICS / NIMS: finish the federal course, then use the language on the fireground.

Career Road tracks ICS-100, ICS-200, ICS-300, ICS-400, IS-700, and IS-800. FireOpsSim points you at the current FEMA Independent Study or instructor-led path, then gives company-level practice so the vocabulary is usable on the next run.

What this requirement actually is

ICS and NIMS credentials are federal/state training products. FireOpsSim does not issue the certificate. Complete the current FEMA or state-delivered course, save the completion record in Career Road, and then practice the same language in size-up, assignments, and radio reports.

  • ICS-100 / ICS-200 and IS-700 / IS-800 are usually free Independent Study courses.
  • ICS-300 and ICS-400 are typically instructor-led through EMI or a state training system.
  • Course letters (IS-100.c, IS-700.b, etc.) change. Always open the current FEMA course page.

Recommended sequence

  1. IS-700 (NIMS) and ICS-100 for every operational firefighter path.
  2. ICS-200 before company-officer / acting-officer work.
  3. ICS-300 / ICS-400 when your department or target rank requires expanding-incident command.
  4. IS-800 when the Task Book or AHJ lists National Response Framework awareness.
