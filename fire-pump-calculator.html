Pump pressure formula

Pump discharge pressure is calculated by adding nozzle pressure, total friction loss, appliance loss, and elevation pressure.

  • PDP = NP + FL ± elevation + appliance loss
  • Friction loss is calculated for the full hose length
  • Use the highest-pressure branch when supplying a wye
