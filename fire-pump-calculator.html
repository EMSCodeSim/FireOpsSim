FireOps Calc FAQ

What does FireOps Calc calculate?

It supports pump pressure practice using nozzle pressure, friction loss, elevation, appliance loss, and line setup values.

Should I use it instead of department SOPs?

No. Use it as a training and reference tool. Department SOPs, instructor guidance, and manufacturer information should control real operations.
