Keep the line clear

Games stay fun. Training stays training.

Firehouse Fun is intentionally separate from task-book completion and formal qualification. Games avoid patient details and should never encourage unsafe, demeaning, or unprofessional behavior. When you want structured development, jump back into the Training Center or Career Road.
