Company drill

Fire pump training drills that make the engineer react to change.

Good pump drills should include communications, changing demand and water-supply decisions—not just setting one pressure.

Five drill patterns

  • Initial attack line, then add a second line without warning.
  • Hydrant supply with a falling residual-pressure trend.
  • Drafting setup with one planted troubleshooting problem.
  • Master-stream deployment that forces a supply decision.
  • Relay evolution with radio communication between pumpers.
