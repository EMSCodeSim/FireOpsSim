EMS life support

BLS, ACLS, and PALS: use the provider your department accepts.

Career Road tracks these as certifications. They are issuer-dependent cards (AHA, Red Cross, or another accepted program), not FireOpsSim courses. This page tells you what to verify, where to enroll, and how to practice around the card.

Source of truth

Your department, medical director, and the card issuer decide which program counts, how often it expires, and whether a skills session is required. FireOpsSim will not substitute for the provider manual or a skills test.

What to do next

  • Confirm which issuer your agency accepts.
  • Enroll in a current instructor-led or blended course if the card is expired or missing.
  • Keep the eCard or paper card with expiration in Career Road evidence.
  • Practice assessment and decision-making separately in EMSCodeSim — that is not BLS/ACLS credit.
