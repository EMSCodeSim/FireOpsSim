Before enrolling

Verify these four things

  1. The course is accepted by the certifying or licensing authority you actually need.
  2. You meet prerequisites before paying or traveling.
  3. The course edition/standard and testing route match your agency or state.
  4. For EMS, confirm program accreditation/approval and your state's licensure pathway.

Why this finder works this way

Fire and EMS pathways change by jurisdiction. FireOpsSim uses authoritative directories and clearly labeled searches rather than pretending one national school list is universally valid.
