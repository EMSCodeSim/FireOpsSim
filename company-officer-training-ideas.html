Company officer

Company officer training ideas that develop judgment and crew leadership.

Use short tabletop decisions, coaching conversations and structured debriefs to practice the parts of the officer job that are hard to learn from a book.

Drill ideas

  • 60-second arrival report from a photo.
  • Assign a three-person crew with limited staffing.
  • Mayday/accountability decision tabletop.
  • Coach a probationary firefighter after a performance miss.
  • Run a five-minute post-incident debrief.
