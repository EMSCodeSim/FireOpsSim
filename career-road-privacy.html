FireOps Career Road

Privacy Policy

Effective August 31, 2026

FireOps Career Road is a local-first professional-growth and career-record app for fire and EMS professionals. This policy describes the personal app experience available in the current iOS release.

Personal career data

Your Career Road, personal Task Books, Quick Log history, credentials, notes, goals, preferences, and locally generated career records are stored on your device. They are not automatically uploaded to FireOpsSim or another service.

Backups, exports, and files

Backup, restore, print, and export actions occur only when you choose them. Backup and restore use the system document picker for user-selected files. Exported or shared files leave the app only when you explicitly choose a save or share destination.

Tracking and advertising

FireOps Career Road does not use your personal career data for advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Photo library

The current iOS release does not request Photo Library permission. Career backup and restore use the system document picker instead.

Sensitive information

Do not enter patient names, addresses, dates of birth, medical record numbers, phone numbers, or other patient-identifying information. FireOps Career Road is not an ePCR, exposure-reporting system, or official patient record.

Retention and deletion

You can erase locally stored career information from Settings using Reset App. Backup or export files that you previously saved outside the app remain under your control and are not deleted by Reset App.

Accounts

The current personal iOS release does not require an account, department login, or demo credentials to use its core features.

Children

FireOps Career Road is intended for professional and career-development use and is not directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Support

For app help, troubleshooting, and support resources, visit FireOps Career Road Support.
