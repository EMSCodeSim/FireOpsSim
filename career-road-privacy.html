FireOps Career Road

Privacy Policy

Effective August 28, 2026

FireOps Career Road is designed primarily as a private professional-growth record for fire and EMS professionals. This policy explains what stays on your device and what may be sent to a department system when you choose to connect one.

Personal career data

Your personal Career Road, personal Task Books, Quick Log history, credentials, notes, goals, and locally generated career records are stored on your device. They are not automatically uploaded to FireOpsSim or ResponderRoadmap. You control exports and backup files that you create or share.

Optional department connection

If you choose to connect FireOps Career Road to ResponderRoadmap, the app sends the information needed to sign in and use your department Task Books. This can include your account email or user identifier, department membership information, assigned Task Book progress, member notes, and evidence or completion information that you explicitly submit for department review.

Department-synced information is used to provide the requested department Task Book, review, approval, and recordkeeping functions. It is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Tracking and advertising

FireOps Career Road does not use your department or personal career data for advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Photos, files, and exports

The app may request access to files or photos only when you choose to select, save, attach, back up, restore, print, or share content. The app does not continuously scan your photo library.

Sensitive information

Do not enter patient names, addresses, dates of birth, medical record numbers, phone numbers, or other patient-identifying information. FireOps Career Road is not an ePCR, exposure-reporting system, or official patient record.

Retention and deletion

You can erase locally stored career information from Settings using Reset App. You can disconnect an optional department connection from the My Department area. Department records that have already been submitted may be retained by the connected department according to its training and records policies.

Children

FireOps Career Road is intended for professional and career-development use and is not directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Support

For app help, troubleshooting, and support resources, visit FireOps Career Road Support.
