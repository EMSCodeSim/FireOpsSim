Why FireOpsSim exists

Designed for real fireground learning, not generic calculators.

Firefighters and driver/operators need fast, practical training tools that match the way pump operations are taught on the apron, in the classroom, and at the pump panel. FireOpsSim connects pump calculators, scenario practice, printable worksheets, and future simulation tools under one fire-focused brand.

The goal is simple: help firefighters practice the math, recognize the layout, understand the why behind the answer, and move from a web tool to a full app when they want more structured training.

Common questions

FireOpsSim FAQ

What is FireOpsSim?

FireOpsSim is the main landing page for firefighter training apps and tools, including FireOps Calc, FirePumpSim, printable pump scenarios, and future fire simulation projects.

Is this for certified driver/operator training?

It is built for education, study, and practice. It should support local instruction, but firefighters should always follow their department SOPs, certification requirements, and instructor guidance.

Why use a separate hub instead of only app store pages?

A hub lets people discover all of the fire tools in one place, helps search engines understand the project ecosystem, and gives instructors a simple page to share with students.
