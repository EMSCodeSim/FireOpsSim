About FireOpsSim

Built from fire and EMS experience.

FireOpsSim is an independent collection of practical training and reference tools created by firefighter and paramedic David Gallaher. The goal is straightforward: give firefighters useful tools at every stage of the career and connect fireground, EMS, training, and wellness resources without unnecessary barriers.
