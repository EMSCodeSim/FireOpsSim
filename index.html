FAQ

FireOpsSim homepage questions

What is the main purpose of FireOpsSim.com?

FireOpsSim.com is the central landing page for your fire projects. It connects FireOps Calc, FirePumpSim, pump calculators, printable scenarios, pump card tools, and future fire training apps.

Why put all tools on the homepage?

A strong hub page helps new visitors immediately see everything available, improves internal linking, and gives search engines a better map of the full FireOps ecosystem.

Should the full calculators live here or on FireOpsCalc.com?

FireOpsSim should provide previews, directories, landing pages, and quick tools. FireOpsCalc.com should remain the deeper calculator/tool site, with FireOpsSim sending users there when they want the full calculator experience.
