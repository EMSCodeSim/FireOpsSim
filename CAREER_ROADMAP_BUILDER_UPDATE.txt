FIREOPSSIM CAREER ROADMAP BUILDER UPDATE
========================================

Updated: certification-tracker.html

New features:
- Target-position selector with Engineer/Driver-Operator, Lieutenant/Company Officer,
  Captain, chief-officer development, instructor/training, HazMat/specialty,
  prevention/inspection, firefighter/EMS leadership, career firefighter, and custom goals.
- Target-date planning with months remaining.
- Personalized Do Now / Next / Later / Maintain-Renew timeline.
- Core-path completion percentage for the selected goal.
- Automatic flagging of current credentials that expire on or before the target date.
- Urgency messaging when a target date is close and core-path gaps remain.
- Saved career goal in localStorage.
- Goal data included in Certification Tracker backup export/import (version 2).
- One-click switch from the target goal to its matching certification path.

Important:
Roadmaps are planning aids only. Departments, states, AHJs, labor agreements,
promotional processes, and certification bodies determine actual prerequisites,
experience requirements, renewal rules, and accepted credentials.
