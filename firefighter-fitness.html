Quick workouts

Get useful work done before the tones drop.

Scale reps, load, and rounds to fit your ability, equipment, and duty status.

10-Minute Bodyweight Burner

  1. 10 air squats
  2. 8 pushups
  3. 10 reverse lunges total
  4. 20-second plank
  5. Repeat at a controlled pace for 10 minutes

15-Minute Stair + Carry

  1. 2 minutes stairs or step-ups
  2. 40–60 seconds farmer carry
  3. 8–12 goblet squats
  4. Rest as needed
  5. Repeat 3–4 rounds

Upper-Body Station Circuit

  1. 8–12 pushups or presses
  2. 8–12 rows
  3. 20–30 second suitcase carry each side
  4. 20–30 second dead hang or grip hold
  5. Repeat 3 rounds

Post-Call Reset

  1. 5 slow breaths
  2. 8 thoracic rotations each side
  3. 30-second hip-flexor stretch each side
  4. 10 bodyweight hinges
  5. 30-second calf stretch each side

Strength + conditioning

Train movement patterns that carry over.

Carry + Hinge Day

Deadlift or kettlebell hinge, farmer carries, rows, sled/tire drag if available, and a short stair finisher.

Squat + Push Day

Goblet/front squat, split squat, pushups or press, loaded step-ups, then easy conditioning.

Pull + Grip Day

Rows or pulldowns, carries, rope pulls if safely available, hangs/grip work, then core anti-rotation.

Work-Capacity Circuit

Rotate stairs, carries, step-ups, pushes, pulls, and floor-to-stand movements at a sustainable pace instead of going maximal from round one.

Mobility + recovery

Stay ready enough to train again tomorrow.

Shoulders + Thoracic Spine

Wall slides, controlled thoracic rotations, gentle lat stretching, and easy band pull-aparts if available.

Hips + Ankles

Hip-flexor stretch, bodyweight deep-squat holds as tolerated, calf stretching, and controlled ankle rocks.

Low-Energy Shift

Easy walk, mobility, light carries, and submaximal strength work can be more useful than forcing another high-intensity session after poor sleep.

After a Hard Call

Hydrate, eat normally, downshift with easy movement and breathing, and avoid treating exhaustion as a cue for punishment exercise.

Fitness boundary: This section is general training information, not individualized medical or exercise prescription. Modify or stop for pain, dizziness, chest discomfort, unusual shortness of breath, or other concerning symptoms, and follow medical, department, and occupational-health restrictions that apply to you.
