Water supply

Firefighter water supply training: know what the source can support.

Practice establishing, evaluating and protecting a water supply as fireground demand changes.

Core decisions

  • Read static and residual pressure in context.
  • Recognize when intake conditions are deteriorating.
  • Plan for longer lays, relay or additional supply.
  • Understand drafting limitations and setup problems.
  • Communicate supply changes before crews feel them.
