Core promise

Make the free site useful enough to come back to.

Free career navigation. A firefighter should be able to see what to learn now and where to go next without creating an account.

Free working tools. Calculators, checklists, drills, references, and practice should solve real station and academy problems.

Free pathways into the ecosystem. When FireOpsCalc, FirePumpSim, or EMSCodeSim is the better tool, send the user there clearly.

Paid products can add depth—not block the basics. The website remains the front door and the career-long reference point.
