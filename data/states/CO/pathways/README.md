# Colorado pathway overlays

These files contain Colorado-specific differences from the national pathways.

Use the national pathway as the complete roadmap, then apply the Colorado overlay for DFPC-specific eligibility, companion certifications, testing, practical requirements, and official resources.

Current Colorado overlay:

- `firefighter-i.json` — Colorado Fire Fighter I

Always verify current requirements with Colorado DFPC and the member's department before testing or claiming qualification.
