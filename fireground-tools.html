What tools should be added first?

Start with high-search, high-utility tools: friction loss table, pump pressure calculator, pump card builder, hydrant flow, relay pumping, and standpipe examples.

Should every tool have its own page?

Yes. Individual pages give Google clearer topics and give users a direct link to the exact tool they need.
