Daily Fire Quiz

Purpose
- Give FireOpsSim a repeat-use daily learning feature similar in spirit to DailyEMSQuiz, but built for the fire service.

Daily experience
- 5-question mixed fire-service quiz
- One stage-weighted question when a Career Stage is saved
- Immediate explanation after each answer
- Daily fire-history fact with source link
- Rotating random fire knowledge card
- Current streak, best streak, completed days and lifetime accuracy saved locally
- Existing Daily Fire Pump Challenge remains a separate math-focused companion

Question mix
- Fire behavior
- Search
- SCBA / Mayday
- Ladders / hose
- Water supply / pump operations
- Apparatus placement
- Building construction / size-up
- Command / leadership / instructor development
- HazMat
- Cancer prevention / wellness
- Fire prevention
- Wildland awareness
- Career development

Source approach
Operational content is written as general training principles and defers to department SOPs, adopted standards, manufacturer instructions and the AHJ. History and health facts link to authoritative sources including USFA/NFA, FSRI, NIOSH and the National Park Service.
