Firehouse Meals

Tasty, practical meals for a crew: easy ingredients, fast cleanup, and recipes that can survive an interrupted dinner. Most are written for 4–6 people and scale up easily.

Station-kitchen tip: Favor meals that can pause safely if tones drop. Keep hot foods hot, refrigerate perishables promptly, and use a food thermometer for meat and poultry. When in doubt, follow current USDA food-safety guidance.
