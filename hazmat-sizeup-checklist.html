HazMat size-up workflow

Use this as a training checklist for first-arriving crews before committing personnel.

  • Confirm location, occupancy, and incident type.
  • Identify product if safe: placard, UN/NA number, container markings, shipping papers, SDS, or facility contact.
  • Approach uphill, upwind, and upstream when possible.
  • Establish command, isolate area, deny entry, and identify hot/warm/cold zones.
  • Consider life safety, exposures, evacuation/shelter-in-place, ignition sources, weather, and runoff.
  • Request HazMat resources, law enforcement, EMS, public works, environmental health, and technical specialists as needed.
  • Document actions, notifications, readings, and zone changes.

Quick field reminders

  • Rescue: only when risk/benefit and PPE support it.
  • Recognition: identify before acting.
  • Isolation: keep people out of the hot zone.
  • Notifications: call for the right help early.
  • Decon: plan before entry when possible.
