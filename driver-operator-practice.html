Suggested training path

  1. Review the basic pump pressure formula.
  2. Practice single handline problems in FireOps Calc.
  3. Move into visual scenarios in FirePumpSim.
  4. Print worksheets for class drills or station practice.
  5. Use pump cards and department setup values to match your local equipment.

Driver/operator FAQ

Is this an official certification question bank?

No. These are original training resources and should be used alongside official materials, local SOPs, and instructor direction.

What topics should I add next?

High-value topics include standpipe operations, foam eductor math, relay pumping, hydrant flow, tender shuttle, and pump troubleshooting.
