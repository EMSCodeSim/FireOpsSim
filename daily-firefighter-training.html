Use a short quiz, one useful fact and one small drill to keep important knowledge available between larger training days.

Simple daily loop

  • Read the history/knowledge card.
  • Choose one missed topic.
  • Run a 5–10 minute skill repetition.
  • Log meaningful progress when it supports a career goal.
