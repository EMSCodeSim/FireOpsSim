Advancement

Firefighter promotion preparation starts before the announcement.

Build knowledge, experience and documented evidence while you still have time to close gaps.

Build four things

  • Target-role knowledge and certifications.
  • Repeated experience performing pieces of the next job.
  • Documented projects, training, leadership and outcomes.
  • Specific STAR-style stories for interviews and evaluations.
