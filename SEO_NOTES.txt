SEO notes for FireOpsSim.com

This rebuild is designed as a search-friendly hub, not just a graphic splash page.

What was added:
- Crawlable HTML links instead of JavaScript-only navigation.
- Unique title tags and meta descriptions for every major page.
- Canonical URLs using https://fireopssim.com/.
- Open Graph and Twitter card metadata.
- JSON-LD structured data for WebSite, Organization, ItemList, SoftwareApplication, FAQPage, and BreadcrumbList where appropriate.
- sitemap.xml and robots.txt.
- Netlify-friendly _redirects and _headers files.
- Supporting landing pages for major search topics: fire pump calculator, FirePumpSim scenarios, driver/operator practice, printable pump scenarios, fireground tools, and apps.

Important next steps:
- Keep adding useful pages, not just buttons. Examples: standpipe pump pressure, relay pumping calculator, friction loss table, foam eductor pressure, hydrant flow calculator, pump card builder.
- Link all related sites together naturally: FireOpsSim.com, FireOpsCalc.com, FirePumpSim.com, and app store listings.
- Add screenshots or short demo videos later, with descriptive alt text and captions.
- Submit sitemap.xml in Google Search Console.
- Ranking is not guaranteed. Google rewards useful content, crawlable structure, clear titles/descriptions, and good user experience over keyword stuffing.
