Use it the right way

This tool does not choose the isolation distance. It only does the math after you get the correct distances from an approved source.

  • Use current ERG/SOP values.
  • Account for wind, terrain, weather, and product behavior.
  • Re-check zones as conditions change.
  • Coordinate evacuation/shelter-in-place through command.
